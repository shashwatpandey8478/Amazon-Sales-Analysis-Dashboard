# 📊 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard

> An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze Amazon product sales, customer reviews, and category-wise performance through dynamic visualizations and KPIs. This dashboard enables business users to monitor revenue trends, identify top-performing products, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard** provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance across multiple product categories. It helps stakeholders understand yearly and quarterly sales trends, product popularity, customer engagement through reviews, and category contributions to total revenue.

The dashboard is fully interactive with slicers and filters, allowing users to explore data across different product categories and quarters.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Amazon sells thousands of products across numerous categories. Analyzing this large volume of sales data manually makes it difficult to identify:

- Best-selling products
- Seasonal sales trends
- High-performing categories
- Customer engagement through reviews
- Revenue contribution by each product

This dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights.

---

## 🚀 Objectives

- Monitor Year-to-Date (YTD) Sales
- Track Quarter-to-Date (QTD) Sales
- Analyze Monthly Sales Trends

# 📂 Dashboard Features

## 1️⃣ KPI Cards

Displays key business metrics:

- 📦 YTD Products Sold
- 💰 QTD Sales
- 💵 YTD Sales
- ⭐ YTD Reviews

---

## 2️⃣ Sales by Month

Line chart showing monthly sales performance to identify:

- Seasonal demand
- Revenue growth
- Peak sales months

---

## 3️⃣ Sales by Week

Weekly sales visualization helps monitor:

- Short-term sales fluctuations
- Weekly growth
- Business performance consistency

---

## 4️⃣ Category Performance Table

Displays:

- Product Category
- YTD Sales
- QTD Sales
- Percentage Contribution

Useful for comparing category-wise revenue.

---

## 5️⃣ Top 5 Products by YTD Sales

Ranks products based on total revenue generated.

Helps identify:

- Best-selling products
- Revenue-driving items

---

## 6️⃣ Top 5 Products by Reviews

Shows products with the highest customer reviews.

Useful for measuring:

- Product popularity
- Customer engagement
- Market demand

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes dynamic slicers for:

- Product Category
- Quarter (Qtr)

These filters instantly update all visualizations.

---

# 📈 Key Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| YTD Sales | Total sales generated during the current year |
| QTD Sales | Sales generated in the selected quarter |
| Products Sold | Total quantity of products sold |
| Reviews | Total customer reviews |
| Monthly Sales | Month-wise revenue trend |
| Weekly Sales | Week-wise sales analysis |
